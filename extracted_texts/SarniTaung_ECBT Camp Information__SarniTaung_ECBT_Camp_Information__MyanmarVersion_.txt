                        Camp Location

- About 7 Miles (11.2 km) away from Thandwe Township.
- Google Map - Latitude 18.425059°N

                       - Longitude 94.465241°E

                  Way & Duration to Camp

- It takes about 45 minutes to Talalnal Elephant Conservation
   Camp from Thandwe Township by car or by motorbike.

- It takes about 40 minutes to Talalnal Elephant Conservation
   Camp from Thandwe Township by boat along Thandwe Chaung.

Compound Area & Number of Elephant

Camp Area         - 2 Acres
