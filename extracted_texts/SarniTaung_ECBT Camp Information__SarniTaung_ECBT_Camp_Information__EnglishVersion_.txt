    Sarni Taung Elephant Conservation Camp
                        Camp Location

- About 6 Miles (9.6 km) away from the Ngapali Beach.
- About 2 Miles (3.2 km) aways from Thandwe Township.
- Google Map - Latitude 18.456816°N

                       - Longitude 94.383428°E

                  Way & Duration to Camp

- It takes about 13 minutes to Sarni Taung Elephant Conservation
   Camp from Ngapali Beach by car or by motorbike.

- It takes about 5 minutes to Sarni Taung Elephant Conservation
   Camp from Thandwe Township by car or by motorbike.

Compound Area & Number of Elephant

Camp Area         - 4.36 Acres
No. of Elephants  - 1 Male Elephant & 4 Female Elephants

                        (Total - 5 elephants)

           Services in Sarni Taung ECBT Camp

 Studying facts and general knowledge about Elephants.
 Seeing the elephants close under guideline by mahouts, feed

   yourself, take photos & video recordings.
 Observing the nature of elephants and their mahouts.
 Donating elephant buffet.
 Taking photography with elephants for your memorial or your

   pre-wedding.
 Enjoying with beautiful local food and special menus.
 Enjoying in the playground.
 Guests can plant for their own memorial.
