                  Palin Elephant Camp
Camp Location

- About two furlongs from the Northern side of the milepost of (9KM)

Nyaung Oo-Pakokku-Myingyan Highway Road

- On the Bank of Ayeyarwaddy River, Near Palin Village

- Google Map   Latitude (N) 21.2158394

               Longitude (E) 94.9728417

Way & Duration for visitation

- Car/motorcycle driving time (15 minutes) from Nyaung Oo/Bagan
- Car/motorcycle driving time (30 minutes) from Pakokku
- Boat trip of Ayeyarwaddy River (1 hour) from Nyaung Oo Jetty

Compound Area & Elephant Number

Camp Area      - 4.18 Acres

Camp Elephants - 2 (male) elephants & 4 (female) elephants

               (Total 6 elephants)
