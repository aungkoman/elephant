Camp Location

   - The Wanet Elephant Camp is located at 5 miles 5 furlongs on the road
      leading to YEL YWAR Hydro Power over the Mandalay - Pyin Oo Lwin
      Expressway 16 miles Transit gate.

   - Inside the compartment (24/25) of Kywat Naphar Reserve
   - Google Map Latitude (N) 21.7845552

                           Longitude(E) 96.2973564

Way & Duration for visit

   - Car/motorcycle driving time (1 hour) from Mandalay
   - Car/motorcycle driving time (1 hour) from Pyin Oo Lwin

               Services in Wanet Elephant Camp

1. Studying the knowledge and facts about Elephants
2. Taking a closer look at the elephant ; Feed them yourself and take free photos

   and videos
3. Observing the nature of tropical forest by riding elephant
4. Donating for the Elephants buffet
5. Learning about elephant at the Elephant's Museum
6. Watching elephants' show(Only in the Weekend)
7. Taking photos with elephants for your memorial events of pre-wedding
8. Enjoying with beautiful scenery and having access to local food and special

   menus
9. Touring the teak plantation near the camp
10.Getting joy from playing in the playground created for children
11.Planting a tree yourself as a memento of your visit
