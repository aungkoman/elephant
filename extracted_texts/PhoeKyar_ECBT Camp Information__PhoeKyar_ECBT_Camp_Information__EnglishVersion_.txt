(A)Camp Location

               Taungngu Township, Taungngu District,Bago Region within saira circle plot
No.82 , Thagarakopin Road Mile point No.(10/2) next to Thay Chaung .From the Yangon ­
Mandalay Expressway milepost (173/1)and the Nay pyi Taw- Yangon Expressway milepost
(173/3) to the west , it is reached by following the road crossing the Thagra Kopin
yoma(4Miles). It is(177)miles from Yangon and (32) miles from Nay Pyi Taw.
Located at estimated map reference(94A/3597410).

(B)Way & Duration for visitation

               - Car/Motorcycle driving time (1hour 20minutes) from Taungngu Township
               - Car/Motorcycle driving time (1hour 20minutes) from Nay Pyi Taw Township
               - Car/Motorcycle driving time ( 20minutes) from Thagara Township
